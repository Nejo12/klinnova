# Klinnova writing quality

## Preserve truth

Keep every supported claim. Never invent facts, names, numbers, dates, quotes, citations, legal conclusions, capabilities, statuses, or promises. Make each retained sentence useful. Preserve current product contracts and terminology in `docs/12-terminology.md`: candidate, employer, vacancy/job, application, match, and exact workflow/status names. Do not swap established terms for variety. Frame automation as outcomes rather than AI features; add AI-branded language only with explicit approval.

## Edit for the audience

- Candidate: plain, respectful, concise, task-oriented; never define people by what they lack.
- Employer: clear, operational, specific; no inflated promises.
- Public/marketing: concrete and credible, differentiated by verified product facts; avoid unsupported claims such as “revolutionary,” “seamless,” or “game-changing.”
- Internal/technical: precise, neutral, direct; retain necessary terminology and implementation detail.

Where useful, reduce staged openings, chatbot wrappers, unnecessary “not X but Y” constructions, dramatic closers, inflated significance, unsupported sales language, vague borrowed authority, filler, repeated sentence openings, forced groups of three, excess dashes, decorative bold, mechanical title-case headings, and stock AI vocabulary. Do not ban normal language mechanically; context and meaning govern the edit.

When editing English or German localization, preserve meaning, product semantics, and status terms. Prefer natural plain language to literal translation when the contract remains exact. An approved Founder writing sample may guide length, punctuation, rhythm, and formality, but never override factual, product, or legal constraints.

## Protected content

Do not automatically rewrite or “humanize” candidate introduction text, employer-authored vacancies, user-authored database content, or submitted applications. Edit an exact item only when explicitly requested and permitted by the product contract.

Do not stylistically humanize Terms, privacy notices, Impressum/legal identity, consent language, employment-law text, or safety/compliance disclosures without explicit authorization for legal-copy editing. Preserve qualified or legally necessary repetition.

If an edit would change product meaning, legal/privacy wording, or approved scope, stop and report the proposed change.
