## 4. Current programme understanding

### 4.1 Funding level

Current official programme material states that GründungsBONUS Plus can support up to **50% of eligible project costs**, capped at **EUR 50,000**.

A request for the maximum grant therefore implies roughly EUR 100,000 in eligible total project costs, subject to the programme's detailed cost rules and the final approved project budget.

### 4.2 Relevant cost categories

Current official guidance identifies categories that may include, subject to detailed eligibility and approval:

- investments/material expenditure;
- running operating expenditure;
- personnel;
- external services/consulting;
- IT development;
- advertising/market-establishment costs;
- certain IP/patent-related expenditure.

No historical Klinnova cost should be assumed eligible merely because it fits one of these categories. Only future costs within an approved, properly delimited project should be considered for the application.

### 4.3 Reimbursement / liquidity implication

The programme operates on a reimbursement basis. The application and financing plan therefore need to demonstrate not only eligible costs but also credible pre-financing/liquidity for the applicant's share and the period before reimbursement.

### 4.4 Personnel constraints to clarify

The current guideline includes specific limits for founder/management and total personnel costs. Exact treatment for a non-incorporated sole proprietor must be confirmed with IBT rather than inferred.

## 10. Current innovation thesis

Do not frame Klinnova as innovative merely because it is an online job board.

The stronger working thesis is the integrated process/business innovation:

1. language accessibility is a first-class matching input, not merely translated UI;
2. candidate access is not dependent on a conventional CV-first funnel;
3. employers state actual language requirements explicitly;
4. matching uses practical eligibility factors and is explainable;
5. private households and local/non-traditional employers can participate under a structured verification model;
6. trust, vacancy legitimacy and moderation are built into the marketplace workflow;
7. Klinnova measures interview/hire outcomes rather than registrations alone.

This thesis must be tested against current competitors before being finalized.

## 11. Social / Berlin-impact working thesis

A defensible current framing is:

> Klinnova aims to reduce avoidable friction between Berlin employers with accessible vacancies and work-authorised jobseekers whose German level or conventional CV profile limits their visibility in mainstream recruitment processes.

Do not claim that Klinnova solves migrant unemployment or guarantees labour-market integration.

Potential measurable impact indicators for the future funded period include:

- participating employers;
- legitimate vacancies;
- completed candidate onboarding;
- applications;
- completed-interview conversions;
- confirmed hires;
- percentage of roles accessible to limited-German candidates;
- vacancy legitimacy / safety incidents;
- employer and candidate funnel conversion.
