# Vision

## Purpose

Stint exists to help capable people become visible to employers and move forward through meaningful work.

## Problem

Many people are legally allowed and ready to work but are filtered out before their ability can be understood. Language barriers, missing local experience, unfamiliar application conventions, incomplete CVs, stale job listings, and unclear employer requirements create avoidable friction.

For newcomers in Berlin, this often means applying repeatedly to unsuitable roles, travelling to interviews that were never realistic, or accepting work far below their capability simply to gain income and local experience.

Employers face the inverse problem: they need reliable people but struggle to identify candidates whose potential is hidden by language, format, or circumstance.

## Core belief

> Everyone deserves to be seen for what they can contribute.

## Mission

Help people move forward through meaningful work.

## Vision

A world where opportunity is determined by potential, not circumstance.

## Brand promise

> One step closer.

Every meaningful interaction should reduce uncertainty, increase visibility, or move a candidate or employer toward a successful match.

## Emotional architecture

- **Seen** is the core emotion.
- **Progress** is the product experience.
- **Dignity** is the human outcome.

## Product thesis

Stint is not a generic job board. It is a trusted employment-access platform that makes accessible opportunities easier to discover, understand, and act on.

The product should help candidates answer:

- Can I actually do this job?
- Is my language level sufficient?
- Is this vacancy still valid?
- What will I earn?
- How far is it?
- What happens after I apply?

It should help employers answer:

- Who is genuinely available?
- Who matches the language and shift requirements?
- Who is reliable and ready to work?
- How can I review and contact candidates quickly?

Employers include private households and residents, shop and retail-store operators, and local organisations — not only registered companies (see D-034).

## Initial market

Berlin, Germany.

The initial audience is people who:

- have permission to work in Germany
- speak English or another language
- speak little or no German
- need accessible, legitimate work
- may not have a German-format CV

Initial sectors include:

- cleaning
- hotel housekeeping
- kitchens and restaurants
- warehousing and order picking
- food production
- moving and event setup
- retail and other operational roles

Initial employer contexts include private homes and apartments, shops and retail premises, and small local businesses hiring for legitimate work.

## Long-term direction

Stint may evolve from a verified job-access product into a broader employment platform with reusable profiles, employer matching, work history, interview support, language assistance, and trusted hiring infrastructure.

The company should expand only after proving that it can improve real hiring outcomes in one market.
