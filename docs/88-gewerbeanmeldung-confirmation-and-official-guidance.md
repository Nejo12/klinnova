## 6. Official Hinweise — legal/administrative effects

The accompanying official Hinweise state, among other things:

### 6.1 Tax-office notification

The Gewerbeanzeige also counts as a notification under **§ 138 Abs. 1 AO** to the tax office responsible for the registered business.

This does not replace the remaining tax-law obligations. Do not infer that tax registration, tax numbers, VAT treatment or other tax matters are complete merely because this notification effect exists.

### 6.2 Statutory accident-insurance notification

The Gewerbeanzeige also counts as a notification under **§ 192 Abs. 1 SGB VII** to the responsible statutory accident-insurance institution.

Do not infer that every follow-up obligation with the relevant institution is automatically complete.

### 6.3 No blanket operating licence

The authority notice expressly preserves other public-law duties and states that the certificate does not authorize the beginning, change, expansion or relocation of a business where a separate permit or Handwerksrolle registration is required.

Current Klinnova documentation must therefore describe the document as a **receipt certification of the Gewerbeanzeige**, not as a general permit proving that every possible regulatory requirement has been satisfied.

### 6.4 Changes that require a new Gewerbeanzeige

The official Hinweise state that material changes can require a new notification under § 14 GewO, including changes such as:

- change of business proprietor;
- change of business activity;
- material extension to activities not customary for the registered type of business;
- relocation of the business;
- closure of the business;
- certain legal-form/ownership changes.

Any future Klinnova operator, activity or establishment change should therefore be checked against the § 14 GewO notification duty before the durable legal record is changed.

### 6.5 Fee / legal remedy

The official notice records the **EUR 15 fee as paid** and contains a one-month objection (`Widerspruch`) period after notification to the Bezirksamt Treptow-Köpenick.

No current factual discrepancy has been identified in this reconciliation that would create an obvious reason to challenge the certificate. This is not legal advice on whether a Widerspruch should or should not be filed.
