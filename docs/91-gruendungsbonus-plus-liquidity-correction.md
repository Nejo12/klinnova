The Founder remains in full-time employment. Personal salary is **not** Klinnova liquidity and must not be booked or represented as if the full monthly salary were available to the business.

The salary is relevant only insofar as it supports the Founder-confirmed ability to contribute approximately **EUR 400/month** to Klinnova without representing private living expenses as business financing.

Do not record salary account details, bank statements, IBAN, BIC, or other personal banking identifiers in Git.

## 5. Consequence for the financial/liquidity model

The financial/liquidity model must be rebuilt from the corrected cash baseline.

The application package must not simply replace EUR 5,000 with EUR 2,000 while retaining expenditure timing that assumed the higher opening balance.

The rebuild must test, rather than assume, whether the approximately EUR 40,000 project envelope is financeable with:

- approximately EUR 2,000 opening liquidity;
- EUR 400/month Founder contributions;
- conservative operating revenue assumptions;
- staged eligible expenditure;
- realistic grant reimbursement timing;
- no Founder salary from Klinnova.

If the rebuilt model falls below zero liquidity or produces an implausibly thin cash buffer, the response must be to adjust expenditure timing, cost envelope, financing sources, or project scope — **not** to fabricate additional Founder cash.

## 6. GründungsBONUS Plus liquidity requirement — working interpretation

Current official-guidance reconciliation already records that the application requires a Businessplan including a multi-year financial/liquidity plan and that overall project financing must be closed and convincingly demonstrated.

This record does **not** introduce a programme rule that applicants must have EUR 5,000, EUR 2,000, or any other fixed minimum opening bank balance. No such fixed threshold is being asserted here.

The relevant application question is whether the actual financing and payment schedule is credible, including the reimbursement-based grant mechanics and the applicant's ability to bridge expenditure until reimbursement.
