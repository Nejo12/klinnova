## 5. Why the initial Nebenerwerb structure is commercially rational

The current side-business structure should be presented positively but accurately.

### 5.1 Risk control

Remaining in full-time employment during the initial validation phase means:

- the Founder does not need immediate Klinnova remuneration for personal living costs;
- the application does not need to fund Founder salary in the base case;
- Klinnova can preserve scarce business liquidity for market establishment and specialist expenditure;
- the Founder can avoid prematurely increasing fixed company costs before recurring demand has been demonstrated;
- transition to full-time entrepreneurship can be based on commercial evidence rather than on receipt of the grant itself.

### 5.2 Commitment is demonstrated through execution, not resignation timing

The Businessplan should avoid the weak formulation that the Founder is "only doing Klinnova on the side."

A stronger formulation is:

> Klinnova is deliberately being established in a staged manner. The Founder remains in full-time employment during the initial market-validation phase in order to protect liquidity and avoid premature fixed costs. The objective is to build a commercially sustainable operating basis and transition Klinnova toward the Founder's main professional activity when objective business milestones justify that step.

This position is compatible with serious entrepreneurial intent while avoiding an unsupported promise to resign on a fixed date.

## 6. Haupterwerb transition model

IBT has previously indicated that a side-business applicant should prospectively intend to operate the funded business as the main occupation and that the Businessplan should show the transition path.

The transition should therefore be expressed as **milestone-based**, not merely date-based.

### 6.1 Stage 1 — controlled validation / Nebenerwerb

Objective:

- establish legitimate employer supply;
- establish candidate demand;
- prove repeatable application/interview conversion;
- validate whether employers perceive differentiated value;
- maintain disciplined liquidity.

Evidence expected from Cohort A/B:

- participating employers;
- legitimate vacancies;
- candidate onboarding;
- applications;
- completed interviews;
- first confirmed hires;
- employer retention/repeat hiring signal;
- early willingness-to-pay evidence.

### 6.2 Stage 2 — commercial validation

The Founder should increase operating commitment when Klinnova demonstrates a repeatable commercial engine rather than isolated beta activity.

Indicative evidence:

- repeated employer demand;
- recurring or repeat paid employer usage after the free-beta phase;
- predictable candidate acquisition channels;
- a measurable pipeline of vacancies/applications/interviews;
- revenue that validates the selected employer-side commercial model;
- sufficient cash visibility to support increased Founder dependence on the business.

### 6.3 Stage 3 — transition toward Haupterwerb

Transition toward the Founder making Klinnova the main professional activity becomes appropriate when the business can support that decision without relying solely on grant proceeds.

The decision should consider together:

1. recurring commercial demand;
2. demonstrated employer willingness to pay;
3. revenue trajectory and gross contribution;
4. forward sales/hiring pipeline;
5. sufficient operating liquidity;
6. expected reimbursement obligations/timing under the grant;
7. workload and Founder capacity;
8. whether continued full-time external employment materially constrains Klinnova's growth opportunity.

No exact resignation date is currently locked.
