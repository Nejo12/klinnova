## 1. Purpose

Record the statutory accident-insurance follow-up that occurred after the Klinnova Gewerbeanmeldung and close the previously open practical question of which statutory accident-insurance institution became responsible.

`docs/88` already recorded that the Gewerbeanzeige also functions as notification under § 192 Abs. 1 SGB VII to the responsible statutory accident-insurance institution. The new VBG correspondence now supplies the institution-specific follow-up evidence.

## 2. Official VBG evidence received

The Founder supplied two VBG letters dated **2026-08-28**.

The letters state that:

- the VBG learned of the business through the competent Gewerbeamt;
- the business belongs to the **VBG (Verwaltungs-Berufsgenossenschaft)** statutory accident-insurance system;
- VBG jurisdiction begins with effect from **2026-09-01**;
- the VBG issued a business/company identifier for the account;
- the business currently has **no contribution obligation because no employees are employed**;
- if the business first employs persons in future, VBG asks to be notified within four weeks;
- self-employed persons / business proprietors are **not automatically personally insured** through this registration and may consider voluntary entrepreneur insurance separately;
- VBG recommends use of its online service (`meine VBG`) and states that a separate Legitimations-ID will follow by post.

The original letters remain private Founder-controlled evidence.

The correspondence distinguishes the company's statutory VBG membership from the proprietor's personal accident-insurance coverage.

No decision to purchase voluntary coverage is recorded by this document.
