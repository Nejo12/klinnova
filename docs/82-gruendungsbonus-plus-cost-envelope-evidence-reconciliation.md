## 1. Executive conclusion

The canonical funding target remains:

- approximately **EUR 40,000 eligible project expenditure**;
- approximately **EUR 20,000 requested grant**;
- approximately **EUR 20,000 applicant share**.

However, the current evidence set does **not** yet justify freezing EUR 40,000 as the final evidence-backed application total.

The first full reconciliation shows three different classes of cost:

1. **Market-plausible specialist / market-entry blocks** where the current planning amount is commercially plausible, but final inclusion still requires provider-specific quotation or equivalent evidence.
2. **Public-price blocks with amount or attribution mismatch** where current public evidence exists but does not support the full planning allocation as currently stated.
3. **Non-submittable planning reserve** where the amount cannot remain as a vague contingency in the final application.

The correct control is therefore:

> Preserve EUR 40,000 as the working ceiling while collecting exact evidence, but do not force the final application to EUR 40,000 by padding, duplicating or arbitrarily reallocating weak costs.

If final evidence supports only a lower total, reduce the project total and corresponding requested grant proportionately.

## 6. Does EUR 40,000 / EUR 20,000 remain viable?

### As a planning target

**Yes.**

There is no need to abandon the EUR 40,000 / EUR 20,000 baseline yet because several large specialist blocks are still only market-rate evidenced and may later receive provider-specific quotes at, below or above the current planning amount.

### As a frozen application amount today

**No.**

Current evidence is insufficient to present all EUR 40,000 as supported expenditure.

The principal reasons are:

- C06 full amount not yet evidenced;
- C09 full amount not justified by necessity/current public prices;
- C10 attribution remains weak;
- C11 cannot remain as contingency;
- C01/C02/C03/C04/C05/C07/C08 still require provider-specific or exact unit-level evidence before final freeze.

### Correct strategy

Keep the EUR 40,000 target while gathering evidence.

Do not lower the baseline prematurely solely because public-market research is incomplete.

But also do not protect EUR 40,000 as a required outcome.

The final application total should be the sum of real, future, project-attributable, evidenced costs that survive the eligibility and liquidity review.
