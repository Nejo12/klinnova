# Product Principles

These principles govern product, design, engineering, operations, and communication decisions.

## 1. See people before profiles

A candidate is more than a CV, accent, passport, or local work-history gap. Stint should make contribution, reliability, availability, and potential visible.

## 2. Progress over perfection

Users should receive value before completing a perfect profile. Ask only for what is needed for the next meaningful step.

## 3. Simplicity over completeness

Prefer a smaller, clearer product over a broad feature set. Every screen should have one primary purpose.

## 4. Trust before growth

Fresh vacancies, verified employers, transparent requirements, and effective moderation matter more than listing volume.

## 5. Reduce friction, not dignity

Simplify applications without making candidates feel reduced, patronised, or treated as disposable labour.

## 6. Make eligibility visible

Language, salary, location, shift, contract, experience, and work-authorisation requirements should be understandable at a glance.

## 7. Mobile first

The candidate experience must work well on a modest mobile device and variable network connection.

## 8. Accessibility is foundational

Keyboard support, semantic HTML, screen-reader compatibility, contrast, readable typography, and large touch targets are requirements, not polish.

## 9. AI is invisible labour

Technology should simplify, translate, classify, and explain in the background. Product copy should describe the outcome rather than advertise AI.

Use:

- Simplify my CV
- Explain this job
- Help me prepare

Avoid:

- AI CV Generator
- AI Job Matcher
- AI Interview Coach

## 10. No dead ends

Empty, loading, error, and rejection states should always explain what happened and identify the next useful action.

## 11. One step closer

The step motif is product behaviour, not decoration. Onboarding, applications, interviews, profile completion, loading, and status changes should reinforce meaningful progress.

## 12. Build vertical slices

Each implemented feature should include the necessary UI, data, validation, authorization, tests, and observability before moving on.

## 13. Collect the minimum sensitive data

Do not collect passports, residence permits, tax documents, or health data unless a validated product need and appropriate legal and security controls exist.

## 14. Human review for consequential decisions

Stint must not make fully automated hiring or rejection decisions. Matching can assist discovery; employers and candidates retain agency.

## 15. Evidence over assumptions

Prioritise features based on candidate interviews, employer interviews, funnel data, and confirmed hiring outcomes.

## Product decision test

Before adding or changing something, ask:

> Does this help someone be seen or take one meaningful step toward work?

If not, defer or remove it.
