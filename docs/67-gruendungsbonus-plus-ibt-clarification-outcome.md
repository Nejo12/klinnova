## 1. Purpose

Record the substantive outcome of the IBT clarification process and convert it into current application-preparation decisions without overstating verbal or written guidance.

## 2. Written IBT response — 25 August 2026

IBT provided the following substantive written clarifications.

### 2.1 Relevant founding date

IBT stated that the relevant founding date is the date of commencement of the registered activity stated in the `Gewerbeanmeldung`.

For Klinnova, the submitted `Gewerbeanmeldung` states:

- commencement date: **01.09.2026**.

Current working consequence:

- **01.09.2026 is the relevant founding date for the GründungsBONUS Plus application chronology.**

This replaces the earlier uncertainty over whether the authority's later confirmation date itself would become the founding date.

### 2.2 Existing MVP / future funded project

IBT did not pre-approve the proposed project boundary.

IBT stated in substance that:

- no final assessment can be made without reviewing the complete application and supporting documents;
- the applicant should assess the proposed separation against the funding guideline;
- the distinction from already-existing activities and/or other projects must be explained comprehensibly in the Businessplan;
- IBT does not perform a preliminary substantive review without the complete application package.

Current working consequence:

Klinnova must maintain a clear documentary boundary between:

**Existing pre-application position**

- functional software MVP already exists;
- historic software-development costs are excluded from the grant request;
- existing production/application capability is evidence of Founder execution, not a claim for retroactive funding.

**Proposed future funded project**

- Berlin market entry and market establishment;
- structured employer and candidate acquisition;
- evidence-driven further product development arising from market validation;
- commercialisation;
- selected external specialist services;
- project-attributable future operating/IT expenditure;
- controlled scaling during the approved project period.

This boundary remains an application argument to be assessed by IBT; it is not pre-approved.

### 2.3 Founder remuneration

IBT stated that a sole proprietorship cannot receive funding for a `Geschäftsführergehalt`, because there is no employment relationship between the sole proprietorship and its proprietor.

Personal living withdrawals are treated as `Privatentnahmen`.

Current working consequence:

- **Founder remuneration is fixed at EUR 0 in the grant cost plan/base financing model.**
- no Founder salary/management salary should be included as an eligible grant cost;
- private withdrawals must remain outside the eligible project-cost calculation.

This converts the previous conservative assumption into the current canonical application position.

## 3. Telephone clarification — Founder-reported, 25 August 2026

The following points were clarified verbally by IBT by telephone and are recorded as Founder-reported verbal guidance rather than written programme statements.

### 3.1 Application timing / pending authority confirmation

Founder report of IBT answer:

- the application/submission date is the relevant start boundary;
- it does not matter that the authority has not yet issued or responded with the final Gewerbeanmeldung confirmation;
- proof that the Gewerbeanmeldung was submitted is sufficient for this procedural point.

Current working consequence:

- provided the stated commencement date of **01.09.2026** has been reached and the submission evidence is retained, the current preparation plan does **not** require waiting for the final authority confirmation before application submission solely for this reason;
- the exact documents accepted by the application portal must still be checked at submission time;
- the application itself remains subject to complete IBT review.

### 3.2 External beta activity / `Vorhabenbeginn`

Founder report of IBT answer:

- external beta activity intended as part of the funded market-entry project should remain on hold until the application has been submitted.

Current working consequence:

Until the application has been submitted:

**Continue**

- Businessplan preparation;
- financial/liquidity modelling;
- cost-plan preparation;
- market/competition research;
- prospect research without contact;
- candidate-channel research without recruitment;
- internal technical audit/maintenance that is not presented as grant-funded project commencement;
- quotation/evidence collection that does not itself create a supplier commitment or begin funded services.

**Hold**

- external employer outreach as part of Cohort A market entry;
- inviting/onboarding external employers into the funded beta;
- publishing real external vacancies as funded market-entry execution;
- active candidate recruitment/invitations as part of funded market entry;
- paid marketing intended for the funded project;
- contractor/service commencement intended for the grant-funded project;
- other external activities that would amount to starting the proposed funded project.

## 4. SaaS / recurring infrastructure — still not specifically resolved

Examples remain:

- hosting;

- monitoring/error tracking;
- other necessary SaaS services.

Current application treatment:

- claim no historical/pre-application costs;
- where possible, evidence the incremental/future project attribution clearly;
- avoid relying on any one uncertain SaaS category for overall project financing;
- final eligibility remains subject to IBT assessment of the submitted application and expenditure plan.
