# GründungsBONUS Plus — C07 Market-Entry & Acquisition Evidence

Status: planning/evidence document only. No external outreach, paid campaign, procurement, contract or application submission is authorized by this document.

## 1. Purpose

This document provides non-binding market evidence for C07 in the current Klinnova GründungsBONUS Plus cost architecture:

- Cost block: employer/candidate market-entry & acquisition
- Current planning ceiling: EUR 6,000
- Geographic focus: Berlin
- Initial market wedge: cleaning-first, with at most 1–2 adjacent low-language-barrier employer segments
- Current execution status: HOLD until the funding application has been submitted

The objective is to determine whether EUR 6,000 is commercially plausible as a controlled market-entry test budget and to define a bounded later quotation/evidence structure.

## 2. Critical boundary

C07 must not be described as a generic marketing budget.

The stronger application framing is a measurable Berlin market-entry work package designed to establish whether Klinnova can:

1. acquire a small first group of employers;
2. bring legitimate vacancies onto the platform;
3. acquire relevant candidates;
4. observe application and interview conversion;
5. identify which acquisition channels can support later commercial scaling.

External Cohort A activity remains on HOLD until application submission under the current Founder/IBT procedural decision.

Internal research, ICP work, channel research, campaign design, budget modelling and unsent outreach preparation may continue.

## 3. Public advertising benchmarks

The following are directional market references, not guaranteed Klinnova performance.

### 3.1 Meta / Facebook / Instagram

Current 2026 DACH/Germany market references indicate approximately:

- CPM: roughly EUR 6–14 per 1,000 impressions in DACH benchmark material;
- CPC: roughly EUR 0.30–1.20;
- other German 2026 benchmark material reports around EUR 0.40 CPC and about EUR 6.80 CPM as a broad average.

Sources:

- https://canemerrant.com/de/blog/facebook-ads-kosten-2026/
- https://recvision.de/blog/meta-ads-kosten-2026/
- https://skill-sprinters.de/blog/social-media/meta-ads-grundlagen/

Important: auction costs vary by audience, objective, creative quality, season, targeting width and campaign maturity. These figures support budget plausibility only.

### 3.2 Google Ads / paid search

A current 2026 DACH benchmark source reports approximately:

- local services: EUR 1.50 CPC and EUR 35–55 CPL;
- B2B/industrial: around EUR 2.50 CPC and EUR 80–150 CPL;
- local-service lead-generation references around EUR 18 CPL in performance-marketing benchmark material.

Source:

- https://ledistagency.de/performance-marketing-benchmarks

For Klinnova, paid search would need narrow intent-based testing. Broad recruitment keywords may be materially more expensive than local-service benchmarks.

## 5. Alternative performance view

At purely illustrative media rates:

- EUR 2,000 of Meta spend at EUR 0.50 CPC would imply about 4,000 clicks before conversion effects;
- EUR 1,500 of search spend at EUR 1.50 CPC would imply about 1,000 clicks before conversion effects;
- EUR 1,000 at EUR 20 CPL would imply about 50 leads.

These are arithmetic illustrations only. They must not be presented as forecast outcomes or promised acquisition volume.

Actual Klinnova conversion may be substantially lower or higher.

## 6. Why C07 should not be one large paid-media line

A EUR 6,000 line labelled only “advertising” would be weak because:

- no channel has yet demonstrated Klinnova CAC;
- the private-beta target is small;
- employer acquisition is likely to require direct founder-led or assisted outreach in addition to ads;
- candidate acquisition may rely partly on community/referral channels;
- excessive front-loaded spend would conflict with the staged liquidity model.

Therefore C07 should be staged and evidence-driven.

## 7. Recommended staged structure

### Stage A — employer-side proof

Objective:

- acquire 3–5 relevant Berlin employers;
- obtain legitimate vacancies;
- validate employer onboarding and publishing friction.

Primary methods after application submission:

- direct founder-led outreach;
- targeted local-service/B2B channels;
- tightly capped paid tests only where useful.

### Stage B — candidate-side proof

Objective:

- acquire approximately 20–30 relevant candidates;
- validate onboarding, matching and application flow;
- observe whether real vacancies produce meaningful applications.

Potential channels:

- paid social;
- paid search;
- community partnerships;
- referral/channel experiments;
- field/local outreach where appropriate.

### Stage C — measured iteration

Only after initial data:

- reallocate budget toward channels producing qualified employers, candidates, applications or interviews;
- pause channels that produce traffic without qualified activity.
