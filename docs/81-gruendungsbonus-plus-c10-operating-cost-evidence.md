# GründungsBONUS Plus — C10 Operating-Cost Evidence

**Status:** F6R / C10 non-binding public-pricing evidence — application preparation only  
**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Planning ceiling:** EUR 1,500  
**Current treatment:** PROVISIONAL / LIKELY REDUCE unless attribution is strong

## 1. Purpose

Assess whether the existing C10 planning block — insurance, communications and general project operating costs — can be supported by current public market evidence without turning ordinary private or general-business overhead into a grant claim.

This record is evidence architecture only. It does not authorize any insurance policy, telecom contract, supplier commitment, payment, external employer/candidate acquisition, paid campaign, Production Sentry activation or application submission.

## 2. Core finding

The EUR 1,500 C10 ceiling is commercially possible across a two-year project period, but price is not the central issue.

The decisive question is whether each cost is:

1. incurred only for the future funded project period;
2. directly attributable to Klinnova business operations;
3. not already an ordinary private/home expense;
4. not duplicated in another cost block;
5. supported by an actual tariff, quotation or policy proposal before final inclusion.

Because those tests are stricter than simple market-price plausibility, C10 remains a lower-confidence block and may need to be reduced or excluded.

## 3. Public market evidence — insurance

### 3.1 Professional / business liability

Current public German 2026 market references indicate that solo self-employed / IT-professional liability can be modest:

- general solo-freelancer professional-liability orientation: approximately EUR 20–40 per month depending on profession, turnover and coverage;
- IT-specific examples can be around EUR 18–35 per month for professional/financial-loss cover, plus low single-digit business-liability components;
- specific comparison portals advertise IT liability from roughly EUR 12 per month.

A reasonable 24-month reference band for a genuinely required Klinnova business-liability package could therefore be approximately:

- EUR 20/month x 24 = EUR 480;
- EUR 40/month x 24 = EUR 960.

This is a market reference, not a Klinnova quotation.

### 3.2 Cyber insurance

Current 2026 German public references show:

- solo-self-employed cyber cover can start around EUR 15–25 per month;
- broader small-business cyber policies can be materially higher depending on turnover, data exposure, security controls and insured limits.

Illustrative low-end 24-month reference:

- EUR 20/month x 24 = EUR 480.

Cyber insurance is not automatically required merely because Klinnova is a digital platform. Final inclusion would require a real project-risk rationale and a quote tied to the actual business profile.

## 4. Public market evidence — communications

### 4.1 Business fixed internet

Current German business internet tariffs provide examples such as:

- Telekom business internet from about EUR 29.95/month net for introductory periods, with higher standard rates depending on product and contract;
- flexible business DSL from about EUR 54.95/month net;
- Vodafone business cable products with introductory rates around EUR 24.90/month and subsequent standard rates around EUR 39.90–59.90/month depending on speed;
- Easybell small-business internet/telephony bundles around EUR 39.95/month net for representative small-company packages.

A 24-month business-internet contract can therefore readily exceed EUR 1,000.

However, that does **not** mean a home internet connection already required for private life becomes fully grant-attributable. A pre-existing mixed-use residential connection is a weak C10 candidate unless a clear incremental business cost can be demonstrated.

### 4.2 Business mobile/data

Telekom business data tariffs currently show examples from approximately EUR 11.30/month net, with larger packages around EUR 22.65/month net and unlimited options materially higher.

A separate project-specific mobile/data service could be evidenced if genuinely required. Existing private mobile service should not be relabelled as a project cost without a defensible incremental-business basis.

## 5. C10 cost architecture

The existing EUR 1,500 planning ceiling should not be presented as one undifferentiated operating-cost line.

Potential final sub-lines should instead be separated as follows:

| Candidate | Potential evidence | Current confidence | Key control |
|---|---|---|---|
| IT professional/business liability | insurer quote / public tariff | MEDIUM | business-specific, future period only |
| Cyber insurance | insurer quote | LOW/MEDIUM | include only if risk rationale is credible |
| Separate business mobile/data | public tariff / quote | MEDIUM | must be incremental and business-specific |
| Separate business internet | public tariff / quote | LOW/MEDIUM | avoid claiming ordinary existing household internet |
| General communications/operating services | exact invoice/tariff | LOW | no vague overhead pool |

## 6. What should be excluded

Exclude from C10 unless a strong project-specific basis exists:

- ordinary household internet already used privately;
- private mobile-phone tariff;
- private insurance;
- utilities, rent or household costs without a programme-supported attribution basis;
- historical insurance or communications invoices;
- generic office/general-overhead reserve;
- duplicated SaaS costs already captured in C06;
- accounting/tax costs already captured in C02;
- legal protection already captured under a clearly separate legal-service justification;
- any cost incurred before the permitted project boundary.

## 7. Evidence required before final inclusion

For every retained C10 item, capture:

- provider/insurer;
- exact product;
- scope/coverage;
- monthly or annual amount;
- net/gross and VAT treatment;
- contract term;
- start date;
- whether the service/account existed before application;
- incremental project-specific cost;
- direct Klinnova project purpose;
- evidence source/date;
- final INCLUDE / REDUCE / EXCLUDE decision.

## 8. Planning implication for the EUR 1,500 ceiling

The current ceiling remains plausible in pure market-price terms, but this is not sufficient for the application.

Example combinations that could reach the amount over 24 months include:

- professional liability at EUR 25/month = EUR 600;
- separate business data at EUR 15/month = EUR 360;
- additional business-specific communications/service costs = remaining EUR 540.

But the final amount must follow evidence, not the ceiling.

If only professional liability is clearly defensible, C10 could end closer to EUR 500–1,000.

If insurance or communication needs are already adequately covered by pre-existing ordinary arrangements, C10 should be reduced further or excluded rather than filled with unrelated overhead.

## 9. Eligibility / tax caveat

This document establishes commercial plausibility only.

It does not establish that every insurance or communications cost is eligible under GründungsBONUS Plus, and it does not determine final VAT deductibility or tax treatment.

Final treatment remains dependent on:

- exact provider evidence;
- actual business-use attribution;
- final application guidance;
- tax/VAT review where necessary.
