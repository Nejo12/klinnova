## 5. Why generic legal-text subscriptions are not equivalent evidence

Current public legal-text services can be very inexpensive. Examples include IT-Recht Kanzlei products around EUR 5.90–9.90 per month plus VAT for configurable privacy/AGB packages.

These services may be useful for standard legal-text maintenance, but they are not equivalent to the C01 scope because C01 is intended to review Klinnova's actual platform model and operational/legal boundaries.

Relevant C01 questions include matters such as:

- exact operator representation for a sole proprietor using the Klinnova business designation;
- platform information duties;
- employer/candidate contractual relationship design;
- legal treatment of verification, moderation and suspension mechanisms;
- privacy roles and processing flows across candidate/employer accounts;
- account deletion / retention / support and data-request processes;
- recruiting-platform liability boundaries;
- employment-related discrimination / AGG risk in platform workflows and matching criteria;
- distinction between a recruitment marketplace/platform and activities that could imply regulated placement/intermediation responsibilities;
- review of user-facing terms and privacy text against the implemented product rather than a generic website template.

Therefore a low-cost generic legal-text subscription should not be used to argue that EUR 4,000 is excessive, nor should such a subscription be presented as evidence for a bespoke EUR 4,000 engagement.

## 6. Proposed Klinnova C01 scope for quotation stage

When it becomes appropriate to request a non-binding provider quote, the scope should be narrow enough to price and evidence.

### Work package A — operator / public legal information

Review:

- sole-proprietor operator wording;
- Klinnova business designation usage;
- DDG information duties;
- Impressum/operator/contact presentation;
- any relevant register/tax information presentation once official data exists.

### Work package B — privacy / data processing

Review actual Klinnova processing for:

- candidate onboarding/profile;
- employer onboarding/verification;
- vacancy publication;
- applications and applicant review;
- email notifications;
- support/privacy requests;
- deletion/retention;
- Supabase/Vercel/mail/monitoring processors where applicable;
- Production Sentry treatment if later activated.

Deliverable target:

- reviewed Privacy Notice;
- processor/subprocessor gap list;
- retention/lawful-basis issues requiring remediation;
- concrete action list.

### Work package C — platform terms and trust/safety

Review:

- candidate/employer terms;
- acceptable-use rules;
- false employer/vacancy controls;
- suspension/termination provisions;
- verification wording;
- limitations of responsibility and user obligations;
- complaint/support process.

### Work package D — employment/recruiting-platform boundary

Review specifically:

- matching criteria and AGG/discrimination risk;
- language and eligibility matching wording;
- work-authorization information handling;
- non-CV-first workflow implications;
- distinction between platform publication/matching and regulated employment placement/intermediation activities, if relevant;
- any legal risk created by future monetisation structure.

### Work package E — remediation memo

Requested output:

- prioritized issue list;
- required changes before broader market entry;
- recommended changes after initial validation;
- items requiring separate employment/tax/privacy specialist advice;
- confirmation of what was **not** reviewed.
