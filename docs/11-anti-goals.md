# Anti-goals

Stint will not become the following during the MVP and Berlin pilot.

## Generic job-board clone

Stint should not optimise for the largest possible number of listings. It should optimise for understandable, current, and accessible opportunities.

## Social network

No follower graph, public feed, engagement bait, or unrelated profile content.

## Staffing or temporary-employment agency

Stint introduces and supports candidates and employers. It does not employ or place workers as a legal temporary-employment provider in the MVP.

## General gig marketplace

Stint will not classify workers as freelancers simply to enable on-demand bookings.

## Payroll, HRIS, or full ATS

These systems are outside the initial problem and would dilute the product.

## Candidate-data aggregator

Stint will not scrape CVs, social profiles, messaging groups, or public job-seeker posts.

## Automated gatekeeper

Matching may rank and explain opportunities, but Stint will not automatically decide who deserves employment.

## AI showcase

The product will not add visible AI features merely for novelty or marketing. Automation must solve a validated user problem.

## Immigration or legal-advice platform

Stint may explain what employers request, but it will not provide individual legal or immigration determinations.

## Nationwide launch before local proof

Berlin remains the initial market until real candidate, employer, interview, and hiring outcomes justify expansion.

## Feature-complete product before pilot

Messaging, reviews, payroll, billing, native applications, advanced recommendations, and other broad capabilities must not delay the first validated hiring journey.
