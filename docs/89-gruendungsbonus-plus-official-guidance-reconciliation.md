## 3. Founding date — resolved

### 3.1 Current 2026 Förderrichtlinie

For `Soloselbständige`, the guideline defines the founding date as the date of commencement of the activity stated in the Gewerbeanmeldung.

Klinnova's authority-certified Gewerbeanmeldung states:

> **01.09.2026**

Therefore the controlling founding date remains:

> **2026-09-01**

### 3.2 Public overview wording conflict

The general programme overview page also gives the `Bestätigungsdatum der Gewerbeanmeldung` as an example of a potentially relevant date when describing the 18-month rule.

That wording is less precise than the 2026 Förderrichtlinie for a sole proprietor and could be read inconsistently.

For Klinnova, do **not** use the authority certification date `26.08.2026` as the founding date.

The 2026 Förderrichtlinie and the case-specific written IBT clarification already recorded in `docs/67` both support `01.09.2026`.

### 3.3 Consequence

- Gewerbeanmeldung evidence is now available and confirmed under `docs/88`.
- the registration-confirmation dependency is closed;
- the founding date does not move to 26.08.2026;
- do not submit before 01.09.2026;
- the company must be founded and have its main seat in Berlin at application time.

## 4. Application/project-start boundary — tighten previous shorthand

The official rule is more exact than the shorthand `after submission`.

### 4.1 Official rule

The 2026 Förderrichtlinie states in substance:

- the application must be submitted before the project begins;
- electronic submission is treated as the application time subject to timely receipt of the legally binding signed form under the stated procedure;
- funding may only be granted for measures that had **not begun when the legally binding signed application was received by IBT**;
- the earliest date from which eligible costs can be recognized is the receipt date of the legally binding signed application;
- an IBT receipt confirmation is **not** a funding approval;
- beginning the project before the funding decision is at the applicant's own financial risk.

The public application page separately says that, at the time of receipt of the legally binding signed application, no supply/service contracts may already have been concluded for funded investments and no claimed running costs may already have arisen.

### 4.2 Current Klinnova operating rule

Replace any casual statement that external Cohort A may start merely because the electronic portal form was submitted with the safer rule:

> **Do not start the proposed funded external market-entry project until the relevant legally binding application-receipt boundary is demonstrably satisfied.**

After that boundary, beginning before the funding decision remains at Klinnova's own financial and eligibility risk; the receipt confirmation is not a grant commitment.

### 4.3 Relationship to Founder-reported telephone guidance

`docs/67` records Founder-reported telephone guidance that the external beta should stay on hold until the application has been submitted.

That remains directionally consistent with the official rule, but the durable execution control should use the more precise signed-application receipt boundary above.

### 4.4 External Cohort A remains HOLD

Before that boundary continue to hold:

- employer outreach intended as funded market entry;
- candidate recruitment intended as funded market entry;
- real funded beta onboarding;
- paid project marketing;
- commencement of contractor/specialist services intended for reimbursement;
- binding grant-dependent procurement.

A separate Founder GO is still required even after the funding boundary is technically satisfied.

**Proposed future funded project**

## 6. Founder remuneration — resolved at EUR 0 for Klinnova

The guideline allows founder/management personnel costs only where the applicable employment/service-contract requirement is met and applies caps to eligible founder/management remuneration.

That generic provision does not create an employment relationship for a sole proprietor.

The written IBT clarification recorded in `docs/67` specifically resolved Klinnova's case:

- the sole proprietorship cannot receive funding for a `Geschäftsführergehalt` for its proprietor because no employment relationship exists;
- personal withdrawals are `Privatentnahmen`.

Current canonical treatment:

> **Founder remuneration in eligible costs: EUR 0.**

Do not reopen this question unless Klinnova's legal structure materially changes.

## 7. Recurring SaaS / infrastructure — partly supported, exact existing-account issue remains unresolved

The official programme clearly lists `laufende Betriebsausgaben` and IT-related expenditure among potentially eligible categories, subject to project purpose, timing and final assessment.

This supports keeping genuinely project-attributable future infrastructure/SaaS as candidate costs.

However, the official material reviewed does **not** specifically resolve the narrower question asked in `docs/63`:

> whether an already-existing service/account can remain in place and only future post-boundary invoices, incremental capacity or newly activated usage be claimed.

Therefore:

- no historical/pre-boundary invoice may be claimed;
- existing-account status does not imply eligibility;
- use exact future-period pricing and project attribution;
- distinguish base services already required by the pre-existing MVP from incremental funded-project use where possible;
- do not make the application financing dependent on uncertain SaaS eligibility;
- final eligibility remains subject to IBT assessment.

No further broad email is required solely on this point. If the final cost plan materially depends on a specific existing subscription, a narrow case-specific procedural question can be asked later with the exact service/timing facts.

## 9. Financing proof — correct an over-strict internal gate

This is a material reconciliation.

### 9.1 At application

The official eligibility criteria require the overall company/project financing, including the requested grant, to be coherent and convincingly presented.

The application also requires a Businessplan including finance and liquidity planning.

### 9.2 At first payout

The official application page states that **proof of closed financing (`geschlossene Finanzierung`) is required with the first payout request**.

Therefore the earlier internal checklist language that treated final closed-financing proof as an unconditional submission attachment was too strict.

### 9.3 Current control

Before submission Klinnova still needs:

- a credible and internally consistent financing plan;
- sufficient liquidity logic for the reimbursement model;
- no fabricated or unsecured financing claims;
- clear treatment of the applicant share and reimbursement lag.

But the durable gate should distinguish:

> **Application-stage financing plausibility and evidence**

from:

> **formal proof of closed financing required no later than the first payout request.**

If the live portal demands additional financing evidence at submission, follow the live portal requirement.

## 10. Reimbursement and tranche mechanics — confirmed

Official guidance confirms:

- reimbursement principle: Klinnova pays eligible expenditure first and submits invoice/payment evidence;
- up to four payout tranches;
- eligible expenditure must fall within the approved period, generally two years;
- each payout tranche should normally represent at least 10% of the approved grant;
- the final payout includes the required use-of-funds/factual report;
- cash-payment receipts are not accepted;
- payments must be traceable through non-cash evidence.

The liquidity model must therefore continue to model reimbursement lag rather than treating the grant as upfront cash.

## 11. Bank-account requirement — record before final submission/payout readiness

The 2026 Förderrichtlinie states that an account of the applicant company must be maintained in Germany. The official application page requests IBAN data and describes grant reimbursement to the company account.

Current consequence:

- do not commit sensitive IBAN/account evidence to the public Git repository;

## 13. Three-year financial-plan format — confirmed

Official guidance states:

- finance/liquidity planning should cover at least three years;
- Year 1 should be monthly;
- later business years may be quarterly;
- the requested grant should be reflected across the two-year approval period in no more than four tranches.

The current durable finance architecture should continue toward exactly this format.

## 14. Berlin commitment after funding — add to long-term control

The official rules require the main seat and any funded Berlin operating site to remain in Berlin and active for at least three years after the final payment.

This is not a current submission blocker, but it is a material post-award commitment and must be understood before accepting funding.

Do not make future relocation/legal-structure decisions without rechecking this funding obligation if Klinnova becomes a grant recipient.

## 15. Public-funding combination — confirmed

The same eligible expenditure may not receive another public grant.

The official application page states an exception for specified publicly financed loans up to 25% of the eligible total, subject to programme rules.

Current treatment:

- no double grant funding for the same cost;
- no assumed public loan in the financing plan unless actually available and compliant;
- any future public-finance combination must be checked against the final application and grant decision.
