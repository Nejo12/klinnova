## 4. Why this allocation is more defensible

### C01 — EUR 4,000

Prior evidence assessed this as commercially plausible for a bounded legal/privacy/platform specialist review. Keep the amount as a quotation ceiling until a Klinnova-specific scope and non-binding quote are obtained.

### C02 — EUR 2,000

Reduce from EUR 2,500 because routine bookkeeping/general administration should not be blended into a specialist funding-project line. Final evidence should separate tax/setup/funding-specific work from ordinary recurring overhead.

### C03 — EUR 3,000

Keep a meaningful UX/accessibility/user-research block while lowering the ceiling. Final inclusion requires deliverables, timing, participant/research scope where applicable, and a quote.

### C04 — EUR 3,500

Retain the prior ceiling because startup-scale security/technical assurance was previously found commercially plausible. Scope must stay bounded; do not convert this into an open-ended penetration-testing allowance.

### C05 — EUR 6,500

Retain most of the previous engineering allocation because external specialist work remains one of the strongest cost blocks. The quote/work package must be future-facing and must not duplicate Founder development work or historical MVP work.

### C06 — EUR 2,000

Reduce materially from EUR 5,000. Existing public-pricing evidence in `docs/72` shows fixed 24-month references for Vercel, Supabase, Sentry and Zoho Mail materially below the prior EUR 5,000 ceiling. The amount may move lower if paid tiers are not actually required or if existing-account treatment is not accepted.

### C07 — EUR 5,500

Retain most of the market-entry allocation because this is central to the proposed funded transition into a validated Berlin market phase. Final evidence requires a channel/unit budget rather than a broad marketing allowance.

### C08 — EUR 1,500

Reduce to control overlap with C07. Final scope should be discrete commercial/brand/content deliverables rather than duplicating acquisition spend.

### C09 — EUR 1,500

Reduce from EUR 2,500. Public equipment benchmarks in `docs/80` show that a capable development workstation package may fit materially below the old ceiling, and necessity remains more important than price. Exact inclusion depends on whether adequate equipment is already available.

### C10 — EUR 500

Strongly reduce. This remains the weakest category because ordinary insurance, household internet, private mobile and general overhead may not be sufficiently project-specific. Retain only separately identifiable future business/project-attributable costs.

4. record net/gross/VAT treatment separately;
5. require project-period timing;
6. prevent duplication between C07 and C08;
7. prevent duplication between Founder work and C05;
8. exclude weak private/general-overhead substitutions;
9. re-test the F4-C liquidity model if payment timing changes materially;
