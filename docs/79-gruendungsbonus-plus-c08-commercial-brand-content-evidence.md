## Planning interpretation

At current public rates, EUR 2,000 corresponds broadly to approximately:

- 3–4 specialist days at a copywriting / communication rate around EUR 500–650/day; or
- about 2.5–3 specialist days at a digital-marketing rate around EUR 700–800/day.

This makes EUR 2,000 commercially plausible for a narrowly bounded work package.

It does not support representing C08 as a full agency rebrand, broad ongoing content retainer or large campaign-production programme.

## Strong C08 scope for Klinnova

A defensible future quotation could cover a combination such as:

1. employer-facing value-proposition refinement;
2. candidate-facing value-proposition refinement;
3. launch / market-entry landing-page copy;
4. concise brand-message hierarchy and tone guidance;
5. employer outreach collateral or one-page commercial asset;
6. bounded launch content templates;
7. copy / content revisions following controlled user or market feedback.

The exact deliverables should be specified before requesting a quote.

## Important separation from C07

C08 must not duplicate the C07 market-entry / acquisition budget.

C07 should cover measured acquisition execution, media spend, acquisition operations and campaign-management activity where justified.

C08 should cover the underlying commercial communication assets and bounded specialist content / messaging work.

A single supplier may theoretically perform work across both categories, but invoices / quotations must identify the deliverables clearly enough to prevent double counting.

## Exclusions

Do not include within C08 without separate justification:

- ordinary Founder-written copy;
- historical Stint / Klinnova MVP branding work;
- work already completed before the eligible project boundary;
- generic social posting with no defined project deliverable;
- paid media spend already classified under C07;
- indefinite marketing retainers;
- complete agency rebranding presented as though EUR 2,000 fully covers it;
- personal Founder branding.
