## 1. Purpose

Test whether the current C03 planning amount of **EUR 3,500** for UX/accessibility/user-research specialist support is commercially plausible using current public market evidence, without treating generic market rates as a Klinnova-specific quote.

## 2. Current C03 planning line

Current planning amount:

> **EUR 3,500**

Intended role:

- independent UX review of the existing Klinnova candidate/employer flows;
- accessibility/WCAG-oriented audit of representative product journeys;
- targeted user-research design and synthesis support;
- prioritized recommendations that can feed the evidence-driven funded-development phase.

The Founder remains the primary product/development owner. C03 is therefore intended for independent specialist review and research capability, not for outsourcing ordinary Founder-owned product work.

## 3. Current public UX rate evidence

Current public German/DACH market references indicate that experienced freelance UX professionals commonly operate around the following ranges:

- typical UX/UI freelance range in Germany: approximately **EUR 60–120/hour**;
- German UPA historical median for self-employed UX professionals: approximately **EUR 92/hour** and **EUR 735/day**;
- current DACH freelancer cross-field average: approximately **EUR 103/hour**;
- current Malt UX benchmark: approximately **EUR 651/day** for experienced UX designers;
- Berlin user-research agency market references commonly cluster around approximately **EUR 80–150/hour**.

These references are market benchmarks only. They are not quotations to Klinnova and do not establish programme eligibility.

## 4. Accessibility-audit market evidence

Current public German/DACH accessibility pricing varies materially by scope and testing depth.

Observed public reference ranges include:

- lightweight/quick accessibility checks from a few hundred euros;
- small professional accessibility audits commonly around **EUR 1,500–4,000**;
- broader WCAG/BITV audit ranges extending higher depending on number of views, complexity, manual testing and reporting;
- specialist accessibility day rates commonly cited above ordinary general-design rates where manual WCAG/screenreader expertise is required.

Low-cost automated or AI-supported scans are not equivalent to a bounded expert accessibility review with manual testing, issue prioritization and implementation-oriented recommendations.

## 5. Plausibility of the EUR 3,500 C03 ceiling

The current market evidence makes **EUR 3,500 commercially plausible** for a tightly bounded specialist package.

Illustrative combinations at current market rates could include, for example:

- 4–5 specialist UX days at roughly EUR 650–735/day; or
- 25–35 hours at roughly EUR 100–140/hour; or
- a smaller professional accessibility audit plus targeted UX/user-research support.

These are planning illustrations only. They are not the final procurement structure.

## 6. Recommended Klinnova-specific quotation scope

A later non-binding quote should be requested against a narrow deliverable set rather than asking for a vague “UX redesign.”

Recommended scope:

### A. Product-flow review

Representative journeys:

- candidate onboarding;
- candidate matching/job discovery;
- candidate application flow;
- employer onboarding/verification;
- vacancy creation/publication;
- employer applicant review/status progression.

Expected output:

- usability findings;
- friction/risk ranking;
- prioritized recommendations;
- clear separation between must-fix, validation hypothesis and optional polish.

### B. Accessibility review

Representative scope:

- keyboard navigation;
- focus order and visible focus;
- semantic structure/landmarks/headings;
- form labels/errors/instructions;
- contrast and non-color information;
- responsive/mobile accessibility;
- screenreader-oriented checks on critical flows;
- WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented issue classification where appropriate.

Expected output:

- issue register;
- severity/priority;
- affected journey/component;
- reproduction notes;
- remediation guidance.

### C. User-research support

Possible bounded support:

- research plan for candidate/employer validation;
- interview/test script review;
- sample task design;
- synthesis framework;
- review of early findings;
- recommendations for product experiments.

The specialist does not need to own all recruitment or execute a large research programme for C03 to be justified.
