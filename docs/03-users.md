# Users

## Primary candidate

A person in Berlin who is legally allowed to work in Germany but has limited German and needs a legitimate opportunity that matches their actual language level, availability, location, and experience.

### Typical characteristics

- speaks English or another language
- German level from none to basic conversational
- may be new to Germany or changing sectors
- may not have a German-format CV
- may need income quickly
- often uses a mobile phone as the primary device
- wants clarity rather than sympathy
- is willing to work but wants to avoid unsuitable or misleading vacancies

### Core needs

- understand whether a role is genuinely accessible
- see salary, shift, location, contract, and language requirements clearly
- apply without unnecessary paperwork
- know whether a vacancy is still active
- avoid scams, unpaid trial work, and exploitative employers
- track what happens after applying
- build a reusable record of experience and reliability

## Candidate segments

### New arrival

Needs first income, local experience, and a simple way to understand the German job market.

### International student

Needs part-time, mini-job, evening, or weekend work that fits study restrictions and availability.

### Skilled newcomer

Has education or professional experience but needs accessible work while pursuing a longer-term career path.

### Career changer

Needs entry into a new sector and may have transferable skills that conventional filters do not recognise.

### Returning worker

Needs flexible or nearby work after a career break, family responsibility, illness, or relocation.

## Primary employer

A Berlin employer with recurring operational hiring needs who is willing to hire people with limited German when the actual role does not require fluency.

### Initial employer types

- cleaning companies
- hotels and hostels
- restaurants and commercial kitchens
- warehouses and logistics companies
- food-production businesses
- moving and event-service companies
- retailers
- small and medium-sized local businesses

### Core needs

- publish vacancies quickly
- state realistic language requirements
- receive candidates who match location, availability, and shift constraints
- identify reliable people beyond conventional CV signals
- reduce unsuitable applications and no-shows
- communicate next steps clearly
- close or expire vacancies without stale listings remaining online

## Administrative user

A Stint operator responsible for trust, quality, moderation, and marketplace health.

### Core needs

- verify employers
- review and moderate vacancies
- investigate reports
- identify duplicates and stale jobs
- suspend harmful accounts
- inspect audit history
- understand candidate and employer funnels

## User relationship

Stint does not rescue candidates or treat employers as adversaries. It creates clearer, safer, and more efficient conditions for both sides to recognise a suitable match.
