# GründungsBONUS Plus — C02 Accounting, Tax, Business Setup and Funding Administration Evidence

Status: working evidence record for application preparation only.

This document supports the current C02 planning block in the Klinnova GründungsBONUS Plus cost architecture.

It is not a supplier quotation, procurement authorization, tax opinion, or eligibility confirmation from IBB/IBT.

## 1. Current planning amount

Current C02 planning ceiling:

- EUR 2,500 total

Current intended scope:

- startup tax setup and ongoing tax/accounting support;
- bookkeeping / EÜR support as appropriate;
- VAT and tax-process clarification;
- bounded business-administration support related to the funded project;
- bounded funding/application administration where separately justified.

The EUR 2,500 amount must not be treated as one undifferentiated “consulting” line in the final application.

## 2. Evidence conclusion

Current public Berlin market pricing makes EUR 2,500 commercially plausible as a combined support block, but not as pure bookkeeping for a very small side-business sole proprietorship.

The strongest structure is a mixed and clearly separated package:

1. recurring tax/accounting support;
2. one-off startup/tax setup and advisory work;
3. bounded application/financial-document administration where genuinely required.

Final inclusion remains subject to:

- Klinnova-specific non-binding quote(s);
- VAT treatment;
- final IBB/IBT eligibility treatment;
- clean separation from ordinary private tax matters;
- clean separation from unsupported generic “funding consulting”.

## 3. Public market evidence — Berlin tax/accounting support

### 3.1 Ronald Haffner, Steuerberater Berlin Mitte — founder side-business package

Public founder pricing observed:

- EUR 69/month for founders operating on a side-business basis;
- stated for monthly revenue or expenditure up to EUR 1,000;
- includes bookkeeping, profit determination, business tax returns and six 30-minute consultations over one year.

Reference:

- https://www.steuerberater-haffner.de/kosten/gr%C3%BCnder-startsups/

Indicative annualized amount:

- EUR 69 × 12 = EUR 828

This is a useful low-end benchmark for a very small side-business tax/accounting package.

It does not by itself substantiate the full EUR 2,500 C02 ceiling.

### 3.2 Ronald Haffner — regular bookkeeping examples

Public bookkeeping examples observed:

- EUR 130/month net for monthly revenue/expenditure up to EUR 3,000;
- EUR 150/month net up to EUR 5,000;
- EUR 200/month net up to EUR 10,000.

Reference:

- https://www.steuerberater-haffner.de/kosten/beispielrechnungen/buchf%C3%BChrung/

Indicative annualized references:

- EUR 130 × 12 = EUR 1,560 net;
- EUR 150 × 12 = EUR 1,800 net;
- EUR 200 × 12 = EUR 2,400 net.

This demonstrates that the C02 ceiling is not inherently excessive once project transaction volume and recurring tax/accounting support rise above the smallest founder package.

### 3.3 b'steuern Berlin — digital tax-advice packages

Public pricing observed:

- from EUR 159/month for a self-starter package;
- from EUR 219/month for a package including ongoing bookkeeping and VAT advance returns.

Reference:

- https://www.bsteuern.com/steuerberater/berlin

Indicative annualized references:

- EUR 159 × 12 = EUR 1,908;
- EUR 219 × 12 = EUR 2,628.

This places the current C02 ceiling inside the range of one year of broader ongoing tax/accounting support.

## 4. Public market evidence — business/funding administration support

### 4.1 IHK Berlin orientation consultation

IHK Berlin currently publishes:

- approximately one-hour orientation consultation;
- EUR 50 plus VAT;
- covers business concept, financing/funding orientation, legal forms, taxes and social insurance at a general-information level.

Reference:

- https://www.ihk.de/berlin/service-und-beratung/existenzgruendung/orientierungsphase/einstiegsberatung-6733098

This is a low-cost public benchmark for general founder orientation only.

It is not equivalent to detailed tax advisory work or preparation of a complete GründungsBONUS Plus application package.

### 4.2 Berlin Secretary — founder/business-plan support

Public pricing observed:

- founder coaching from EUR 45/hour plus VAT;
- business-plan services advertised from EUR 450 plus VAT;
- a separate expert-opinion service advertised from EUR 90 plus VAT.

Reference:

- https://www.berlin-secretary.de/businessplan-preisgestaltung/

This provides a low-to-mid market reference for bounded founder/business-plan administration work.

It should not be treated as evidence that Klinnova requires these exact services.

### 4.3 IBB / Berlin advisory-program benchmark

The current IBB Förderfibel 2026/2027 states that a separate advisory-funding program may recognize consultation costs up to EUR 3,500, with subsidy rates depending on location/program conditions.

Reference:

- https://www.ibb.de/media/dokumente/publikationen/wirtschaft-in-berlin/foerderfibel/foerderfibel-2026-2027-interaktiv.pdf

This is not a Klinnova quotation and must not be confused with GründungsBONUS Plus eligibility.

It does, however, provide a current public-policy benchmark showing that professional business consulting projects in the low-thousands of euros are not unusual.

## 5. Plausibility against the EUR 2,500 ceiling

Several realistic market combinations remain below or around the current C02 ceiling.

### Scenario A — lean side-business accounting + bounded setup

Illustrative only:

- small founder tax/accounting package: ~EUR 828/year;
- one-off specialist setup/advisory work: ~EUR 500–900;
- bounded application/financial-admin support: ~EUR 300–600.

Indicative combined corridor:

- approximately EUR 1,628–2,328 before final VAT treatment.

### Scenario B — broader recurring tax/accounting support

Illustrative only:

- recurring accounting/tax package: ~EUR 1,900–2,400/year;
- limited one-off setup or project advisory: ~EUR 300–600.

Indicative combined corridor:

- approximately EUR 2,200–3,000.

Under this structure, the current EUR 2,500 ceiling is commercially plausible.

## 6. Recommended Klinnova-specific scope for later quotation

A future non-binding quote should be requested against a bounded scope rather than the phrase “accounting and tax support”.

Suggested scope:

### A. Initial tax/business setup

- review of sole-proprietor tax setup after Gewerbeanmeldung;
- VAT treatment discussion for the funded project;
- input on whether gross or net amounts should be used in the application planning;
- review of deductible operating-expense categories relevant to Klinnova;
- separation of business/project expenses from private expenses.

### B. Recurring accounting support

- bookkeeping support for project-related transactions;
- EÜR support;
- VAT advance-return support if applicable;
- business tax-return support where in scope;
- reconciliation of project invoices/payments needed for reimbursement evidence.

### C. Funding administration support

Only if genuinely required:

- review of expenditure documentation structure;
- reconciliation between cost plan, financing plan and bookkeeping categories;
- support preparing reimbursement evidence from actual invoices/payments;
- no duplicate charging for work already completed internally.

## 7. Exclusions

C02 should not include:

- the Founder's personal income-tax return unless clearly and legally attributable to the business project;
- private tax planning;
- unrelated personal financial advice;
- bookkeeping for pre-application historical MVP costs;
- generic grant-writing retainers without a defined project deliverable;
- costs already incurred before the eligible project boundary;
- duplicate work already covered under C01 legal review or other cost blocks.
