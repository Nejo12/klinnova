## 13. Financing-gap interpretation after this model

The earlier nominal comparison of EUR 20,000 applicant share versus EUR 14,600 Founder-controlled 24-month contribution capacity produced a EUR 5,400 residual gap before revenue.

This F6G model demonstrates why that nominal gap alone is not the correct cash test:

- operating revenue may contribute after validation;
- grant reimbursements recycle liquidity during execution;
- expenditure can be staged;
- timing matters more than comparing two static totals.

However, the model also shows that early months remain sensitive to reimbursement delay. Therefore no conclusion should be drawn that external financing is unnecessary under every timing case.
